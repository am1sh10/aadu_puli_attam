Aadu Puli Aattam

Three tigers hunt fifteen goats on a 23-point board. The goats win by trapping every tiger. The tigers win by capturing five goats.

A traditional strategy game from South India. Learn it step by step, solve puzzles, or play the computer or a friend.

You play as

Your side sits at the bottom of the screen; the computer or the other player takes the other side. Goats move first. You can flip the board any time.

No sound on iPhone or iPad? Check that the silent switch is off and the volume is up.

How to play

  • Goats move first. Each turn, place one goat on any empty point. Goats cannot move until all 15 are placed. After that, a goat moves one step along a line to an empty point.
  • Tigers start on the apex and the two points beside it. A tiger steps along a line to an empty point, or jumps over a goat right next to it to the empty point directly behind, capturing that goat.
  • Capturing is mandatory. If a tiger can jump, a tiger must jump. One jump per turn, no chains.
  • Tigers win by capturing 5 goats. Goats win when no tiger can step or jump.
  • If the same position appears three times, the game is a draw. After 30 moves each without a capture, either player can claim a draw.
  • Tap a piece, then tap where it goes. In Guided help, a red ring marks a goat a tiger can capture, a dashed ring shows where the tiger would land, and the number beside each tiger counts its moves.
